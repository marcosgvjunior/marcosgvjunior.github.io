## Purpose

The content model turns professional evidence into a navigable portfolio. It avoids a
single page that tries to act as biography, README, project list, publication
record, and blog at the same time.

## Primary Pages

- `index.md`: positioning, featured portfolio evidence, and current
  case-study directions.
- `pages/case-studies.md`: selected project and field-note cards.
- `pages/research.md`: education, awards, and selected publications.
- `pages/writing.md`: writing lanes and professional positioning.
- `pages/about.md`: short biography and contact links.

## Data Files

- `_data/case_studies.yml`: case-study records with title, track, status,
  summary, signals, images, and links.
- `_data/research.yml`: education, awards, and publications.
- `_data/writing.yml`: writing themes and evidence sources.
- `_data/navigation.yml`: primary site navigation.

## Case-Study Criteria

A project is strongest as a case-study page when it has:

- a clear problem statement;
- a source, dataset, artifact, or repository link;
- a method section that explains the workflow;
- validation evidence or tested behavior;
- limitations that keep claims measured;
- links to public repositories, papers, posts, or demos.

## Quality Standard

Each page makes the professional signal easy to understand: what was built, why
it matters, what evidence supports it, and where the reader can inspect the
source material.
